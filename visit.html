YOUR FIRST NIGHT
01

Arrive

Meet the Sergeant-at-Arms and members before the gavel. No awkward wandering around pretending to text.

02

Observe

Watch prepared speeches, impromptu speaking, evaluations and the mechanics of a formal meeting.

03

Participate — only if invited and comfortable

You are not required to give a prepared speech on your first visit. The point is to experience the club.

04

Connect

Ask questions after the meeting, meet members and decide whether you want to return.

GUEST FAQ
Do I have to speak?

No prepared speech is expected from a guest. Depending on the meeting, you may be invited to participate in a short impromptu segment, but you can decline.

How long is the meeting?

Club 1600 meetings follow a structured agenda. Exact finishing time can vary by programme; the current agenda should be checked when you RSVP.

Can I attend online?

Hybrid attendance is available. Contact the club before the meeting for current access details.

Can I visit more than once?

Yes. Visiting more than once is the easiest way to understand the culture before deciding on membership.
