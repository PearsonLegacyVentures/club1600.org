LEADERSHIP OUTSIDE THE CLUB

DYNAMIC YOUTH IN ACTION · SUMMER 2026

Six weeks.
Skills that travel.

Over six weeks, Club 1600 members worked with young people through Dynamic Youth in Action, an organization serving at-risk youth. The sessions focused on practical development — entrepreneurship, communication and job readiness — taking the same skills practised inside Toastmasters into a room where they could matter immediately.

01ENTREPRENEURSHIP

See opportunity. Build value.

Introducing young people to entrepreneurial thinking, problem-solving and the discipline of turning ideas into practical value.

02COMMUNICATION

Own your message.

Helping participants express ideas clearly, speak with confidence and understand how communication shapes opportunity.

03JOB READINESS

Walk in prepared.

Practical preparation for the workplace: presentation, professionalism, communication and the habits that influence first impressions.
