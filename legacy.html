The legacy
keeps moving.

Club 1600's story began before Bahamian Independence and continues through each member who takes the floor.

THE TIMELINE
1968

The first meeting.

Twenty-four men gathered at St. Benedict's Hall in December 1968.

1969

Officially chartered.

The First Bahamas Branch joined Toastmasters International as Club 1600.

1973

A nation steps forward.

As The Bahamas entered Independence, Club 1600 was already developing communicators and leaders.

2021

Echoes of History.

The club's story reached the big screen in a documentary preserving decades of institutional memory.

2025

Legacy Leadership.

Past presidents and generations of service were honoured at the Legacy Leadership Banquet.

2026

Leadership in motion.

The 62nd presidential term begins — carrying the institution into its next chapter.
