01 / JOHNATHAN ALBURY

Confidence
built through practice.

Johnathan joined to strengthen his communication skills. Today, he serves as Club 1600's Treasurer for 2026–2027.

“

WHY I JOINED

I originally joined 1600 because I knew that I was suffering as a communicator. Every successful leader I knew had mastered the art of communication, so it was an important skill set for me to have.

WHAT HAS CHANGED

Since joining, I've seen growth in my confidence and my ability to speak for myself and others. It has also helped me gain new opportunities to speak about the importance of public speaking to young professionals.

Johnathan AlburyMEMBER ACCOUNT · LIGHTLY EDITED FOR READABILITY
02 / CHARLES ROSE

From the boardroom
to a better punchline.

Charles joined Club 1600 in 2024. He talks about getting his ideas across at work, explaining results to others—and improving his jokes.

“

WHY I JOINED

I joined in 2024 to learn public speaking skills and how to communicate. A lot has changed since then. My communication skills have greatly improved, and now I can get my ideas across to almost anyone.

WHERE IT SHOWS UP

Effective communication is key in the situations I face every day—from presenting figures and results in the boardroom to training stakeholders on the use of a report or product. I could not have gotten as far without the knowledge I gained from this club.

ONE MORE THING...“My jokes are funnier.”
Charles RoseMEMBER ACCOUNT · LIGHTLY EDITED FOR READABILITY
03 / DEANGELO JOHNSON

Stepping outside
his comfort zone.

Deangelo joined Club 1600 in 2026 to become more confident speaking, grow as a leader at work and in his personal life, and build friendships with other ambitious men.

“

WHY I JOINED

I joined Club 1600 because I wanted to step out of my comfort zone and learn the true art of communication — to be bolder when I speak and become a better leader in my personal life and at work.

THE BROTHERHOOD

I also wanted to be part of a brotherhood, learning alongside men who want the best out of life, building my network, and wearing a lot of bad boy suits on a Thursday.

04 / AMAR PEARSON

The training ground
for bigger stages.

Amar first visited Club 1600 in November 2024 after an invitation from Azano Major, then Vice President Membership and now Club President. As an entrepreneur, he relies on the skills he practises at the club every day.

“

WHY I JOINED

I joined Club 1600 in November 2024 after being invited by Azano Major, then our Vice President Membership and now our President. When I attended my first meeting as a guest, I knew this was the training ground I needed to achieve my leadership goals.

WHAT HAS CHANGED

Since joining, I've amplified the communication and leadership skills I use every day as an entrepreneur — especially my ability to speak off the cuff in almost any situation.

BEYOND THE CLUB

Thanks in part to the training I've received here, I've been called on to speak at schools, churches and conferences, appear on the news, and even win pitch contests.

WHAT COMMUNICATION MEANS TO ME“Communication is the glue that takes people of all backgrounds from ordinary to extraordinary. And there's no better place to learn it than Club 1600.”
Amar PearsonMEMBER ACCOUNT · LIGHTLY EDITED FOR READABILITY
