MEMBER STORY · CLUB 1600

Meet Johnathan
Albury.

Johnathan joined Club 1600 knowing that communication was a skill he needed to strengthen. He now serves as the club's Treasurer for 2026–2027.

“

WHY I JOINED

I originally joined 1600 because I knew that I was suffering as a communicator. Every successful leader I knew had mastered the art of communication, so it was an important skill set for me to have.

WHAT HAS CHANGED

Since joining, I've seen growth in my confidence and my ability to speak for myself and others. It has also helped me gain new opportunities to speak about the importance of public speaking to young professionals.

Johnathan AlburyIN HIS OWN WORDS · LIGHTLY EDITED FOR READABILITY
