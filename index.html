02 / OUR LEGACY

A BAHAMIAN INSTITUTION

The story began
before Independence.

Twenty-four men gathered at St. Benedict's Hall in December 1968. The First Bahamas Branch received its Toastmasters charter in 1969. More than five decades later, the room is still open.

1968

The first meeting.

Twenty-four men gather at St. Benedict's Hall. A new chapter in Bahamian communication and leadership begins.

1969

Club 1600 is chartered.

The First Bahamas Branch becomes part of Toastmasters International — and the foundation for a growing movement in The Bahamas.

2021

Echoes of History.

The club's story reaches the big screen, documenting a legacy that stretches across generations.

2025

Legacy honoured.

Past presidents and decades of service are celebrated at the Legacy Leadership Banquet.

2026

The next chapter.

The 62nd presidential term carries the institution forward — with the next generation already in the room.

05 / ECHOES OF HISTORY

A STORY THAT DESERVED TO BE TOLD

More than five decades.
One film.

Commissioned and directed by Past President Shacoy Mullings, DTM, Echoes of History traces Club 1600 from its birth in 1968 through Majority Rule, Independence and the modern Bahamas. Nearly 40 interviews and 20 hours of footage were distilled into a one-hour documentary.
