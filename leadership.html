62ND PRESIDENT · 2026–2027

Azano P. Major

President, First Bahamas Branch of Toastmasters Club 1600.

THE PRESIDENT

Set the direction.
Raise the standard.

Azano leads the club's 62nd presidential term under the year's leadership focus: ascending higher, empowering others and inspiring the next generation. His role is to align the executive, protect the club's culture and keep the membership moving toward measurable growth.

THE EXECUTIVE LINEUP
01PRESIDENT

Azano P. Major

Toastmasters International defines the President as the club's chief executive: setting tone and direction, supervising club operations, leading business and officer meetings, establishing goals, delegating effectively and keeping the club on track.

02VICE PRESIDENT EDUCATION

Daniel Douglas

The VPE is the second-ranking officer and owns the educational program: planning and organizing meetings that meet members' learning needs, supporting progress toward education goals and leading the Club Education Committee.

03VICE PRESIDENT MEMBERSHIP

Anthony A. Bodie Jr.

The VPM leads membership growth and retention: attracting guests, helping convert qualified prospects into members, supporting new members and directing the Club Membership Committee.

04VICE PRESIDENT PUBLIC RELATIONS

John C. Fowler

The VPPR develops and directs the club's publicity program, protects the Toastmasters brand, keeps members and the public informed, manages communication channels and builds positive awareness that supports membership.

05SECRETARY

Mandella Campbell

The Secretary is responsible for club records and correspondence: accurate minutes, notices, rosters, governing documents and the official administrative record of club and executive committee activity.

06TREASURER

Johnathan Albury

The Treasurer manages the club's financial policies, procedures and controls: receiving and disbursing approved funds, meeting financial obligations, maintaining accurate accounts and reporting regularly to the club and executive.

07SERGEANT-AT-ARMS

Amar Pearson

The SAA is responsible for club property management, meeting-room preparation and hospitality. In practice, that means ensuring the physical environment, equipment, protocol and guest experience are ready before the meeting begins.
