Your first 90 days
should not be random.

A simple progression from new member to active participant.

WEEK 01

Get oriented

Confirm your Toastmasters account, understand Pathways, meet the executive and learn how Club 1600 runs.

See the meeting flow →
WEEK 02–03

Take a support role

Start with Timer, Lexicologist/Grammarian or another role that teaches you how the meeting works from inside it.

Open Role Lab →
FIRST 30 DAYS

Choose a mentor

Find someone who will help you prepare, review speeches and understand the club faster than trial-and-error will.

FIRST 45 DAYS

Deliver your Ice Breaker

Your first prepared speech is not supposed to prove you are polished. It establishes a baseline.

Speech toolkit →
DAY 45–75

Rotate through roles

Evaluate, facilitate, time, observe language and practise impromptu speaking. Variety accelerates growth.

DAY 90

Review the evidence

What improved? What still makes you uncomfortable? Choose the next three skills intentionally.
